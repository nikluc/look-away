# Look Away

A first-person horror prototype. A hand comes out of the dark and points. Look any other way, but only after it points. Turn too soon, too late, or the same way, and she takes you.

**Play in the browser:** https://nikluc.github.io/look-away/ (desktop browser recommended; the first load is about 100 MB)

- Mouse, or arrow keys / WASD, to look
- Pick from the ouija board with the planchette: PLAY ALONE, or PLAY WITH OTHERS (up to 10 players around the pit, last one standing; open a table and share the 4-letter code)
